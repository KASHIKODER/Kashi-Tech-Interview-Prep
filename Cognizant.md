## Question: What do you know about Cognizant?

Cognizant is a global technology and professional services company that was founded in 1994 and is headquartered in Teaneck, New Jersey. It helps enterprises modernize their applications, processes and technology through services such as digital engineering, cloud, data and AI, cybersecurity, automation and consulting.

The company is currently positioning itself as an AI Builder, focusing on converting AI investments into practical and measurable business outcomes. Its purpose is to engineer modern businesses to improve everyday life.

I also learned that Cognizant strongly emphasizes values such as working as one, raising the bar, innovation, integrity and taking ownership. These values, along with its current focus on AI-led software engineering and workforce development, make it an attractive organization for someone beginning a technology career.

## Question: Why do you want to join Cognizant?

I want to join Cognizant for three main reasons.

First, Cognizant provides the opportunity to work on enterprise-level technology across areas such as software engineering, cloud, data and AI. My current experience is in developing a full-stack learning management system, and I want to take my software development fundamentals to a larger, professional environment.

Second, Cognizant is investing significantly in developing AI-ready talent and creating roles such as Frontier Certified Engineers. This shows that the company is not only adopting new technology but is also preparing its employees to work effectively with it.

Third, I connect with Cognizant’s values, particularly “Own it” and “Raise the bar.” I like taking responsibility for my work, learning from problems and continuously improving the quality of what I build.

Therefore, I see Cognizant as a place where I can contribute with my current fundamentals while developing into a dependable software professional.

## Question: Tell me about recent developments at Cognizant.

One recent development that interested me was Cognizant’s announcement that it plans to scale its workforce to 5,000 Frontier Certified Engineers and 10,000 Frontier Business Operators.

I understood this as part of Cognizant’s AI Builder strategy. The company is focusing not only on providing AI tools but also on developing professionals who can understand client problems, build AI-enabled solutions and remain responsible for the results in production.

I also read about Cognizant expanding its collaboration with Google Cloud around Gemini Enterprise. These developments show that Cognizant is actively investing in both AI technology and employee capabilities.

## Question: Which Cognizant value do you relate to?

The Cognizant value I relate to most is “Own it.”

For me, ownership means taking responsibility not only for completing an assigned task but also for understanding its purpose, checking its quality, communicating problems and ensuring that the final result works properly.

While working on my project, Kashi Learning, I learned that building an application involves more than implementing individual features. I had to understand the complete flow between the frontend, backend, database and real-time functionality. That experience helped me develop a stronger sense of responsibility toward the final outcome.
