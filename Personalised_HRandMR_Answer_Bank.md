## 16. What is the difference between a team leader and a manager?

A manager generally has broader responsibility for planning, resource allocation, deadlines, performance and alignment with organizational goals.

A team leader works more closely with the team’s day-to-day execution. The leader guides members, removes blockers, coordinates tasks and helps the team achieve its immediate objectives.

In some organizations the responsibilities may overlap, but a simple difference is that a manager focuses more on the overall system and outcomes, while a team leader focuses more directly on guiding the team’s execution.

## 17. What qualities should a good team leader have?

A good team leader should have clear communication, technical or functional understanding, fairness, accountability and the ability to support team members.

The leader should set clear expectations, listen to problems, provide constructive feedback and take responsibility for the team’s result. A good leader should also recognise the strengths of different members instead of trying to handle everything personally.
